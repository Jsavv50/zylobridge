## 1. Visual Philosophy

Zylobridge’s design system embodies a premium, modern, trustworthy, and enterprise-capable aesthetic tailored for high-trust professional engagements across global and African markets. 

---

## 2. Design Tokens & Standards

- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif hierarchy using Inter typography tokens, explicit font weights (`font-medium`, `font-semibold`, `font-bold`), and high-contrast text pairs (`text-foreground` against `bg-background`).
- **Color Palette**: Deep slate/charcoal foundations combined with high-contrast accent tokens and semantic indicators for verification badges, financial escrow states, and milestone progress.
- **Surface & Elevation**: Subtle shadows over heavy borders, rounded cards (`rounded-xl`), and responsive container padding (`container mx-auto px-4`).
- **States**: Meaningful empty states with illustrative icons, skeletons for loading feedback, and clear toast error alerts.
